# Hand meshes

The left and right rigged hand meshes come from the Immersive Web
[WebXR Input Profiles assets](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/tree/main/packages/assets),
distributed under the MIT license in the adjacent `LICENSE.txt`.

Pinned download sources (`@webxr-input-profiles/assets@1.0`):

- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@webxr-input-profiles/assets@1.0/dist/profiles/generic-hand/left.glb
- https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@webxr-input-profiles/assets@1.0/dist/profiles/generic-hand/right.glb

The original GLBs are unmodified. The application poses the skeletons and
replaces their material with procedural leather gloves at runtime.
